Dragon Boat

For the past six years, I have been a member of Paddlers Anonymous DaWave.

For the past two years, I have had the privilege of coaching the team.

My responsibilities include season planning, budgeting, scheduling practices, collecting fees, managing waivers, organizing team events, developing practice plans, and maintaining team morale.

I've been fortunate enough to help lead a team of ~50 paddlers into silver and gold medals across canada - Montreal, Toronto, Vancouver, to name a few - and I look forward to earning more together.
